# Free startup launch platforms and directories to launch or list a new software product (BetaList, Uneed, Peerlist, Fazier, TinyLaunch, StartupBase and more)

Scope: general launch platforms and startup directories. AI-tool-specific directories are covered by a separate route (https://tracert.site/routes/free-ai-tool-directories). Hacker News is skipped (covered elsewhere).

## TL;DR — fastest path
- **Free with no badge and no long queue:** Product Hunt (free; personal account, not a company account) and Peerlist Launchpad (weekly launch on Mondays; free per a third party, since Peerlist's help center doesn't state the cost; needs a Verified Peerlist profile and a 100%-complete project). Launching Next is free with a plain form and daily review, but there is no stated wait time and it is not clear the site is still active.
- **Free but slow, or you must link back:** Uneed's free line gives a launch date at least 50 days out, and you need an upvote score of 10 to stay published (20 for the do-follow link). StartupBase's free tier takes about 4–5 weeks and requires the StartupBase badge on your site. Fazier Basic takes up to 30 days and requires a link to Fazier on your homepage or footer. TinyLaunch's free queue time isn't stated, and its do-follow link needs the embedded badge.
- **Paid-only, or treat as paid:** BetaList ("All submissions are paid", with a full refund if you aren't selected; prices only appear at the end of the form). Microlaunch's pricing and submit pages show only paid tiers (from $39 with a code). Third-party sources disagree on whether a free queue still exists.
- **Developer tools only:** DevHunt runs weekly launches. Its own About page claims the free queue averages 6 months. The skip-the-queue price conflicts between DevHunt's pages ($49 vs $19), so check it at checkout.
- **Skip for launch reach:** SideProjectors (a marketplace and showcase with no visible dates and no audience numbers) and Indie Hackers products (403 to automated fetches; a third party says the links are nofollow).
- **Needs a human:** every site needs an account (Google/X/LinkedIn/email) or a form with a name and email, and paid tiers need payment. Nothing here can be finished read-only.

### betalist.com — paid-only now; good audience, but budget for it
- **Access** (2026-11-07, observed): the homepage, /faq and /submit loaded. /pricing returns 404.
- **Rules** (2026-11-07, observed, FAQ): "All submissions are paid. There is no free submission option." If your startup isn't selected, you get "a full refund automatically". Pre-launch and already-live startups both qualify, but the startup must be "new to our audience". Products that are "already widely known or have had significant press coverage" aren't a fit. You need your own domain, so free hosting subdomains and direct app-store links are refused.
- **Pricing**: the FAQ says "Current plans and prices are shown at the end of the submission form" (2026-11-07, observed). The page lists no amounts. Third-party: SaaSCity (updated 2026-10-07) says the prices appear only after you sign in. Older third-party guides (Flowjam and others) describe a free 2–3-month route and a $129 expedited option. Treat those as outdated, since the first-party FAQ now says there is no free route.
- **Backlink/audience** (2026-11-07, first-party claim): featured startups get "a do-follow backlink from a DR 76 domain". The FAQ claims "500,000+ pageviews a month", and says "Faster plans" guarantee a newsletter spot to "70,000+ subscribers". Review time depends on the plan and is shown when you choose one.
- **How** (2026-11-07, observed): /submit is now an agent-assisted flow. It drafts a submission and sends a link to "review and pay". You confirm your email, pick a plan, and "nothing is published until you do". New users get an account created; existing users log in with an emailed code. Google and X sign-in are also offered.
- **Alive** (2026-11-07, observed): the homepage shows featured startups under Today (November 7th), Yesterday (November 6th) and Thursday (November 5th). Some listings are labelled "BOOSTED".
- **Gotchas**: there is no free tier, and payment is not a guarantee, because selection still applies (refund if rejected).

## Prep checklist
- Your own domain. BetaList refuses free subdomains and app-store links, and that's a sensible default everywhere.
- A live landing page you can edit, in case you take a free tier that needs a badge or link back (StartupBase, Fazier Basic, TinyLaunch do-follow).
- Logo, tagline, short and long description, tags or categories, screenshots, and optionally a demo video.
- Accounts: Google, X or LinkedIn for StartupBase. Google or email for Fazier. A personal (not company) account for Product Hunt. A Verified Peerlist profile with the project 100% complete.
- A plan for upvotes on Uneed (score 10 to stay published, 20 for do-follow) and for launch-day engagement on the leaderboard sites.
- A launch calendar: Uneed free is 50+ days out, StartupBase free about 4–5 weeks, Fazier free up to 30 days, DevHunt free a reported 6-month average, Peerlist on Mondays.
- A budget decision on whether to pay. BetaList has no free option at all.

## Needs a human
- Creating or signing in to accounts on every platform, and getting a Peerlist profile verified.
- Submitting any form, including Launching Next, which asks for the submitter's name and email.
- Paying for any tier (BetaList is mandatory paid). Checking checkout prices and currency behind sign-in (BetaList, DevHunt).
- Adding a badge or link to the product's site for free tiers that require it.
- Deciding whether to rely on Launching Next, SideProjectors or Indie Hackers products, whose liveness or reach couldn't be confirmed.
